📖 The full story

Sri Lanka opened its first comprehensive national battery testing centre, the Centre for E-Mobility and Battery Technology, at the National Engineering Research and Development Centre on Monday (Nov. 23). The inauguration was held under the patronage of Minister of Science and Technology Prof. Chrishantha Abeysena. Attendees included Ministry Secretary Dr. Roshanie Dissanayake, NERDC Chairman Dr. Chithral Ambawatte, Acting Director General Manoj Karunaratne and representatives of local and international industries.

The facility was established with a total investment of approximately Rs. 161.5 million, comprising Rs. 118 million from the Government's National Quality Infrastructure Strengthening Project and Rs. 43.5 million contributed by NERDC. It can assess battery performance, capacity, durability and safety under different environmental conditions, and also tests electric vehicle charging equipment and various battery systems.

The centre aims to expand Sri Lanka’s battery testing capabilities beyond conventional assessments to include modern battery technologies, supporting the development of electric mobility and renewable energy sectors. It will provide specialised testing services to local manufacturers, importers, researchers and technology developers, while supporting domestic product development and increasing local value addition.
