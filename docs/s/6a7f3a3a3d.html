2027 Budget second reading passed by 97-vote majority

Economy📍 Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-21 · 📰 Reported by 8 outlets

📌 What happened

Parliament passed the second reading of the 2027 Budget by a 97-vote majority following a seven-day debate. The vote concluded with 157 votes in favour and 60 votes against. This is the 81st Budget in the history of Sri Lanka.

📖 The full story

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake presented the proposal on November 12 in his role as Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development. Debate began on November 13 and finished at 6.30 pm on November 20, marking the third budget presented by the National People’s Power government. Records show 157 MPs voted in favour, 60 MPs voted against, and no MPs abstained.

The 2027 Budget estimates total government spending at Rs. 7.933 trillion, up from Rs. 7.309 trillion in revised 2026 estimates. Total revenue is projected at Rs. 6.15 trillion, while revenue and grants reach Rs. 6.18 trillion. These figures leave an estimated budget deficit of Rs. 1.753 trillion, representing 4.5% of Gross Domestic Product.

Alongside these proceedings, Central Bank Governor Dr. Nandalal Weerasinghe announced headline inflation will likely exceed 9% this month. In judicial matters, Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama ordered a progress report on the Krrish inquiry by December 01, while MP Chamara Sampath Dassanayake was further remanded until November 27.
