Miracle on the Mississippi: All accounted for after tanker collides with cruise ship

Society📍 New Orleans, United States 🇺🇸 · 2026-11-14 · 📰 Reported by 6 outlets

📌 What happened

A tanker called Atlantic Asphalt hit the docked river cruise ship American Heritage on the Mississippi River in New Orleans at about 12.30pm on Friday. Rescue crews pulled people from the water as the cruise ship slowly rolled onto its side, and 24 patients were admitted to hospital. The river was closed to all vessel traffic while responders worked.

📖 The full story

The tanker Atlantic Asphalt collided with American Cruise Lines' American Heritage at about 12.30pm on Friday while it was docked on the Mississippi River in New Orleans, said Kimberly Curth, Port of New Orleans press secretary. The cruise ship had about 129 passengers plus crew onboard and had docked about two hours earlier to begin passenger disembarkation.

New Orleans firefighters, port and city police, the US Coast Guard and emergency medical staff worked to pull people from the water, and mayor Helena Moreno's office said multiple individuals had been rescued. Video showed tugboat crew pulling people in lifejackets or holding flotation rings from the river, and those who got off were brought inside the port's administration building. LCMC, which runs University Medical Center, said 24 patients were admitted, Acadian Ambulance took eight people to hospital with injuries not yet known, and the Coast Guard closed the river to all vessel traffic between mile markers 93 and 96.

Video showed the American Heritage slowly roll onto its side, and witness Jamie Holford told WDSU it took 15 minutes from impact to rolling over, calling it surreal. Maritime law expert Martin J Davies of Tulane University told WVUE the tanker was lucky not to strike the Crescent City Connection bridge, which carries about 160,000 cars daily. Officials gave no cause; one emergency source said the tanker may have lost power, unconfirmed, and American Cruise Lines said guests and crew were evacuated without giving numbers.

The American Heritage is a 300ft paddle-wheel ship built in 2015, previously the Queen of the Mississippi, with more than 80 staterooms; the Atlantic Asphalt is an oil tanker built in 2017, about 160 meters long, deadweight 17,764 metric tons, flying the Marshall Islands flag, owned and commercially operated by Nave Shipping SA, a company registered in Liberia, with maintenance, technical and safety work overseen by Singapore-based Thome Ship Management. It is not on international sanctions lists checked by services including Magic Port, but Canadian authorities fined it C$7,800, recorded 11 August 2020, after an officer failed to properly supervise a transfer operation. Rescue activity scaled back later on Friday, and Louisiana governor Jeff Landry said he was headed to New Orleans.
