Sri Lankan Embassy issues special advisory to citizens in Bahrain

Society📍 Bahrain 🇧🇭 · 2026-11-19 · 📰 Reported by 2 outlets

📌 What happened

The Sri Lankan Embassy in Bahrain has issued a special advisory urging Sri Lankan nationals living in the country to remain vigilant due to the prevailing regional situation. Nationals are asked to track official instructions from local authorities and follow safety measures. The Embassy has also provided contact numbers for nationals who need emergency help.

📖 The full story

The Sri Lankan Embassy in Bahrain told Sri Lankan nationals residing in the country to stay alert because of the prevailing regional situation. The mission asked all Sri Lankans there to monitor official guidelines and warnings issued by Bahraini authorities and observe safety instructions.

Emergency telephone lines were released for Sri Lankan nationals requiring assistance. Nationals can reach Attaché G.M.D. Tharangika at (+973) 3597 0290 or (+973) 3510 6301, or contact Labour Clerk Gothami at (+973) 3371 2875.

Sri Lankans in Bahrain have been advised to stay informed through official channels. They are urged to contact the Embassy if an emergency occurs.
