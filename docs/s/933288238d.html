Denmark expels Russian diplomat over alleged plot to kill Ukrainian rapper

Politics📍 Copenhagen, Denmark 🇩🇰 · 2026-11-21 · 📰 Reported by 4 outlets

📌 What happened

Denmark expelled Russia's defence attaché after accusing Moscow of plotting to kill Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner at a concert near Copenhagen. Police arrested three men with firearms and knives five minutes before the scheduled performance, which was cancelled. Two Danish men were charged with attempted murder and assisting a foreign intelligence service.

📖 The full story

Denmark summoned the Russian ambassador and announced the expulsion of Russia's defence attaché within seven days, with Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen stating that they have exposed Russia. He also said Denmark is considering suspending Schengen visas for Russians and is working with the EU to tighten visa rules.

PET assessed that Russian intelligence planned to kill US-Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner, whose real name is Albert Viktorovich Vasiliev, during a concert on 25 September in a Copenhagen suburb. Police stopped three men in a car near the venue north of Copenhagen with two loaded pistols and two knives at about 9.55pm, five minutes before the concert; one had bought a ticket, and the concert was cancelled, while a fourth suspect was arrested later. The suspects are linked to the banned gang Loyal to Familia, and two Danish men were charged under Section 108 with assisting a foreign intelligence service and attempted murder, denying wrongdoing.

PET head Rasmus Kieffer-Kristensen said the assassination attempt is not something previously seen in Denmark and shows Russia is increasingly aggressive and risk‑taking, adding that Russian intelligence attempted to carry out an execution in Denmark. Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen stated that Denmark faces a cornered Russia and must not waver in supporting Ukraine. European Council President Antonio Costa called the alleged attempt unacceptable and promised the EU's full backing against Russian hybrid attacks.

Russia's Foreign Ministry Maria Zakharova said Moscow will give an appropriate response, while the Russian embassy called the expulsion vengeful and claimed no evidence of Russia's involvement. Copenhagen has previously accused Russia of hybrid attacks, including a drone shutdown of Copenhagen airport in September 2025. PET also said in September that Russia was recruiting Danish citizens to help plan sabotage against Danish defence firms with ties to Ukraine, and Russia has denied involvement in such operations.
