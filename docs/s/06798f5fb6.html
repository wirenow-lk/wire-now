Two Sri Lankan workers walking home killed by cargo protruding from truck in South Korea

Society📍 Hwaseong, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea 🇰🇷 · 2026-11-28 · 📰 Reported by 4 outlets

📌 What happened

Two Sri Lankan workers were killed after being struck by a cargo truck in South Korea. The accident happened while they were walking home along a road without pavement after finishing work at a nearby factory.

📖 The full story

Two Sri Lankan workers in their 30s were killed in a road accident in Hwaseong, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea. They were walking home after finishing work at a nearby factory when a cargo truck's protruding roof struck them.

The accident occurred at around 9.10 p.m. on November 26 on a road with no pavement, forcing pedestrians to walk along the roadside. A section of a wooden house roof was protruding from the passing cargo truck when it hit the workers, killing them both.

The truck driver told police he had failed to properly observe the road ahead. Investigations revealed he was not under the influence of alcohol or drugs at the time of the accident, and South Korean police have taken legal action against him while investigating whether cargo-loading regulations were violated. The identities of the deceased Sri Lankans have not yet been publicly disclosed.
