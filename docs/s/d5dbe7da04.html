Rs. 8.4 million bribery case : Bail denied for Chamara Sampath

Politics📍 Colombo, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-20 · 📰 Reported by 3 outlets

📌 What happened

The Colombo Magistrate's Court rejected the bail application of former Minister and Parliamentarian Chamara Sampath Dassanayake, who is in remand custody over an alleged Rs. 8.4 million bribery case involving three private companies in the phosphate trade.

📖 The full story

Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama issued the order when the case was taken up for hearing today (20). Dassanayake was brought before the court earlier today for the hearing.

The allegations state he solicited and accepted Rs. 8.4 million in bribes from three private companies involved in the phosphate trade. The bribes allegedly relate to the granting of licences to private companies for phosphate-related business activities.

  • Former Minister and Parliamentarian Chamara Sampath Dassanayake denied bail

  • Bribes linked to granting phosphate business licences
