Saudi-led coalition intercepts two missiles, two drones in Riyadh

Politics📍 Riyadh, Saudi Arabia 🇸🇦 · 2026-11-15 · 📰 Reported by 2 outlets

📌 What happened

Saudi-led coalition forces intercepted two ballistic missiles targeting Riyadh early Sunday, following the interception of two drones in Riyadh and southern regions on Saturday. The coalition also intercepted several drones aimed at Yemen's Marib city. Fighting intensified across multiple Yemeni governorates with heavy casualties and displacements reported.

📖 The full story

On Sunday, coalition spokesperson Turki al-Maliki posted on X that air defences intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched by the Houthi terrorist militia toward Riyadh. On Saturday, al-Maliki said two suspected Houthi drones were intercepted and destroyed in the Riyadh and southern regions.

Saudi authorities say at least 26 people have been killed in suspected Houthi drone and missile attacks in the kingdom since September 23. The Houthis have repeatedly targeted Riyadh's King Khalid International airport, the East-West pipeline, and oil infrastructure in eastern and southern Saudi Arabia. The coalition also said it intercepted several drones launched towards the Yemeni city of Marib.

Yemeni army spokesman Colonel Majid al-Nuzaili said government forces made gains against the Houthis in al-Hazm and Madghal districts of al-Jawf as fighting intensified in Marib. He said 321 Houthi leaders were killed or wounded in 723 targeted operations by Saudi-backed forces in Taiz, Lahij, Marib and al-Jawf governorates, and 117 military vehicles used by the Iran-allied rebel group were destroyed. The coalition carried out at least 37 air strikes, 56 drone attacks, and 541 artillery strikes.

Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree said the Saudi-led coalition launched 72 air strikes against the Houthis in Saada, Taiz, Lahij and al-Jawf governorates over the past 24 hours. Saree said the Houthis targeted Yemen's Saudi-backed forces in the Kharaz area of Lahij governorate with ballistic missiles. Houthi-affiliated media reported two women and two children injured from artillery shelling by Saudi-backed forces in a residential neighbourhood of the Shuaub district in Sanaa. More than 340,000 people have been displaced since August according to the International Organization for Migration. The US Embassy in Riyadh issued a nationwide security alert on Saturday urging Americans to exercise heightened vigilance, citing increased threat of Houthi attacks on civilian airports and critical infrastructure causing civilian casualties and severe travel disruptions, warning hostilities could spread rapidly.
