Heavy showers above 100mm expected in several provinces today

Weather📍 Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-27 · 📰 Reported by 6 outlets

📌 What happened

Sri Lanka’s Department of Meteorology forecast heavy showers above 100 mm in parts of the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, Southern and Uva provinces on 27 November 2026. It also expected showers in the Northern, North-central and Eastern provinces and thundershowers elsewhere after 1.00 pm. The public was advised to take precautions against temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

📖 The full story

The forecast was issued at 05.30 a.m. on 27 November 2026 by the Department of Meteorology. Misty conditions may prevail in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and Central provinces and in the Galle, Matara and Badulla districts during the early hours of the morning. Several spells of showers will occur in the Northern, North-central and Eastern provinces, while showers or thundershowers will occur at several places elsewhere in the island after 1.00 pm.

Heavy showers above 100 mm are likely at some places in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, Southern and Uva provinces. The general public is requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

Showers or thundershowers may occur in sea areas off the coast extending from Mannar to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle, Matara and Hambantota. Showers or thundershowers will occur in several other sea areas around the island during the evening or night. Winds will be north-easterly or variable in direction, with speeds of 25-35 kmph.

Wind speeds may increase up to 40-45 kmph at times in sea areas off the coast extending from Kankesanthurai to Puttalam via Mannar and from Trincomalee to Matara via Batticaloa and Hambantota. These sea areas will be fairly rough at times, while other sea areas around the island will be moderate. Temporarily strong gusty winds and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
