Sudanese army air attack kills 13 in western Sudan: Rights group

Politics📍 Abu Qalb, West Kordofan, Sudan 🇸🇩 · 2026-11-16 · Al Jazeera

📌 What happened

The Sudanese Armed Forces launched an air strike that killed at least 13 civilians in the Abu Qalb area of West Kordofan. The victims included women and children, and several homes were destroyed.

📖 The full story

The Sudanese Armed Forces, or SAF, launched an air strike. The strike killed at least 13 civilians in the Abu Qalb area of West Kordofan state, and the victims included women and children, according to the Emergency Lawyers rights group.

The warplanes from the military government attacked the Abu Zabad locality. The strike happened the day before Monday, and Emergency Lawyers said several homes were destroyed in the strike, which they described in a social media post on Monday after preliminary monitoring, but Al Jazeera was unable to immediately verify the report.

Emergency Lawyers said the continued use of indiscriminate airstrikes on residential areas poses a direct threat to civilian lives and is a grave violation of international humanitarian law. The group documents human rights violations and cited videos of horrific scenes of destruction. Mohamed Didan, the official spokesperson for the Special Operations Forces that fight alongside the SAF, said on Facebook that his troops moved through Abu Zabad and eliminated RSF positions in West Kordofan.

The war between the army and the RSF began in April 2023 and has created one of the world's worst humanitarian crises, according to several aid organisations. Reports say the war has killed at least 59,000 people, displaced about 13 million and pushed parts of the country into famine, while much fighting is concentrated in Darfur and Kordofan and recently shifted towards the Blue Nile province. Drone warfare has surged during the war and become the deadliest threat to civilians, experts said both sides are being supplied with drones by countries in the Middle East and beyond, and the SAF has gained control over several strategically important villages and localities in West Kordofan recently.
