Russia launched a major overnight attack across Ukraine, targeting bridges in Kyiv and railway stations with ballistic missiles. Key crossings in the capital were hit during the nationwide assault. At least nine people were killed.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky described the strike as one of the most massive of the war so far. The attack came days after Ukraine badly damaged the strategically important Crimean Bridge. Russian President Vladimir Putin had threatened to retaliate for that earlier attack on the bridge.

The Kremlin built the Crimean Bridge to link Russia and the occupied peninsula. The latest strikes took place during a flurry of talks about the war. They also came as the Kremlin pushed back against a US claim that Russia could be open to Western-backed security guarantees for Ukraine.

BBC reporter Sarah Rainsford was in Kyiv after the night of strikes to report on what happened.
