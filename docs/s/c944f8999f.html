📖 The full story

The source is Newswire. The Committee on Ways and Means held discussions. The meeting discussed Sri Lanka’s tariff policies and the progress of ongoing bilateral and multilateral trade agreements.

The meeting was chaired by MP Vijesiri Basnayake. It focused on the current status of trade agreements and measures to advance negotiations. Acting Director General of the Department of Commerce, Somasena Mahadiulwewa, briefed the committee on the country’s trade policies and agreements.

The committee stressed the need to conduct feasibility studies on trade agreements currently under negotiation. It also wanted steps to ensure that discussions progress. Deputy Ministers Chathuranga Abeysinghe and Eranga Weeraratne, along with several MPs, attended the meeting.
