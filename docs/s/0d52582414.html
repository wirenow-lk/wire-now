LAUGFS Gas gets cheaper after Trade Ministry intervention : New price announced

Economy📍 Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-17 · 📰 Reported by 2 outlets

📌 What happened

LAUGFS cooking gas cylinder prices have been cut after the Ministry of Trade intervened. A 12.5kg cylinder drops by Rs. 745, from Rs. 6,740 to Rs. 5,995. A 5kg cylinder falls by Rs. 322, from Rs. 2,720 to Rs. 2,398.

📖 The full story

Minister of Trade Wasantha Samarasinghe announced that LAUGFS gas cylinder prices have been reduced. He said the cuts were made after the Ministry of Trade intervened.

A 12.5kg cylinder now costs Rs. 5,995, down from Rs. 6,740, a reduction of Rs. 745. A 5kg cylinder falls from Rs. 2,720 to Rs. 2,398, a reduction of Rs. 322.

According to the Minister, the revised prices provide relief to consumers purchasing LAUGFS domestic cooking gas cylinders.

💡 Why it matters

The lower prices give relief to consumers who buy LAUGFS domestic cooking gas cylinders.
