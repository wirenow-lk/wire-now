Two fishermen killed after being hit by train in Trincomalee

Society📍 Cod Bay Fisheries Harbour area, Trincomalee, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-23 · 📰 Reported by 2 outlets

📌 What happened

Two fishermen died and a third person was badly hurt after a train hit them in the Cod Bay Fisheries Harbour area of Trincomalee on the night of the 22nd. The three were standing on the railway track when a train going from Trincomalee to Colombo Fort struck them. One died at the scene and another later died in hospital.

📖 The full story

Police said three people were standing on the railway track when a train travelling from Trincomalee to Colombo Fort hit them in the Cod Bay Fisheries Harbour area of Trincomalee on the night of the 22nd. One person died right at the spot.

The other two were taken to Trincomalee Hospital with serious injuries. One of them later died while being treated, bringing the death toll to two.

The two dead men were both fishermen, aged 48 and 58, from the Matara and Dondra areas. Their bodies were placed at the Trincomalee Hospital mortuary.

The China Bay Police are looking further into what happened.
