📖 The full story

The Attorney General’s Department disclosed fresh findings in the investigation against former State Intelligence Service (SIS) Director, retired Major General Suresh Sallay, over allegations of concealing information and misleading investigations into the 2019 Easter Sunday attacks. At Colombo Fort Magistrate’s Court, Additional Solicitor General Dileepa Peiris said investigations established that Sallay and Azad Maulana, who appeared in a Channel 4 documentary on the Easter attacks, stayed with their families at the same hotel in Ahungalla on August 15 and 16, 2020.

Peiris said Sallay first claimed the encounter was coincidental but later changed his position when questioned about telephone records. The Additional Solicitor General argued the circumstantial evidence lent credibility to certain claims made by Maulana. He also alleged that an informant known as Thambi Anna provided military intelligence with information regarding Zahran Hashim six months before the Easter Sunday attacks, and that the relevant file was concealed with Sallay’s knowledge.

Investigators are also looking into the possible involvement of a former Deputy Inspector General of Police. The court was told that a 31-page report by a five-member specialist medical panel, appointed to examine allegations of torture against Sallay while in detention, had been received. Peiris argued Sallay reported psychological stress but did not disclose physical torture to the medical panel.

Bhanuka Mallawarachchi, appearing for Sallay, disputed the prosecution’s interpretation and said the medical report referred to a degrading physical examination of his client. The defence also pointed out that the medical panel recommended further investigations into alleged mistreatment during detention. Peiris told the court that, in his opinion, sufficient evidence existed against former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa to name him as a suspect, but any decision to arrest him rested with investigating officers; after considering submissions from both parties, Colombo Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasena ordered the case taken up again on January 27, 2027, for a report on the progress of investigations.
