No tsunami threat to Sri Lanka following 6.8 magnitude earthquake off Timor-Leste

Weather📍 Banda Aceh, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-21 · 📰 Reported by 5 outlets

📌 What happened

A 6.8 magnitude earthquake hit the Indian Ocean near Banda Aceh on Saturday (21). Sri Lanka’s Department of Meteorology said there is no tsunami threat to the island. The National Tsunami Early Warning Center said the quake happened at about 3:12 PM on November 21, 2026.

📖 The full story

The earthquake was recorded at a depth of 156 kilometres. Its epicentre was at latitude 8.04° South and longitude 127.57° East. The National Tsunami Early Warning Center gave the time as approximately 3:12 PM on November 21, 2026.

After the quake, the Department of Meteorology issued a No Tsunami Threat Bulletin at 3:30 PM. The bulletin told people in coastal areas around Sri Lanka about the situation. It confirmed that the earthquake does not pose a tsunami threat to Sri Lanka.

The advisory was issued to keep coastal communities informed after the seismic event in the Indian Ocean. The report came from Newswire.
