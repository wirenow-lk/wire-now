President Donald Trump held a White House press conference on Friday as a test for possible 2028 successors. He asked JD Vance and Marco Rubio to stay still for 90 minutes while he rambled about potholes. The room was crowded, the air‑conditioning hissed, and a sound engineer adjusted the microphone, giving Trump an echo.

Trump referenced his trilateral meeting with China and Russia, likening it to the Yalta conference and noting that the United States, Russia and China control 92% of the world’s nuclear arsenal. He also mentioned his uncle John Trump, an MIT electrical engineer, and claimed a genetic blessing from that background. Rubio then defended the president’s outreach to those leaders, arguing that their combined nuclear power makes regular dialogue essential and dangerous to ignore.

A reporter asked which of the two would make a better president after Trump, and Trump swatted the question away without answering. Vance replied that he would support the president’s Iran policy even though he opposes the war, while Rubio smiled knowingly. The event was described as the first audition for the 2028 election and the article expects further careful positioning in the coming campaign.
