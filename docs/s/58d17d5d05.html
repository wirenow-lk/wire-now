A moment of silence took place in Parliament today (27) for those who lost their lives and those injured because of Cyclone Ditwah. Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne made the announcement in Parliament. The Speaker said it was held to remember people affected by the natural disaster.

The event marked one year since Cyclone Ditwah battered Sri Lanka on November 27, 2025. Members of Parliament, along with the Speaker, stood up to observe a moment of silence for the victims.
