Arrest warrant issued for Rajangane Saddharathana over failure to appear in court

Politics📍 Colombo, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-25 · 📰 Reported by 3 outlets

📌 What happened

The Colombo Fort Magistrate's Court issued an arrest warrant for Rajangane Saddharathana on the 25 after he failed to appear in court. The case involves alleged social media statements said to be detrimental to religious harmony. Magistrate Pasan Amarasena also ordered notices to be issued to his bail sureties.

📖 The full story

According to Newswire, the Colombo Fort Magistrate's Court issued the warrant on the 25 because Rajangane Saddharathana did not appear before the court. The case concerns alleged statements on social media that were said to harm religious harmony. Magistrate Pasan Amarasena made the order.

The court acted after receiving submissions from the Criminal Investigation Department. It also ordered that notices be issued to Saddharathana's bail sureties. The case has been adjourned to February 24, 2027, for the Government Analyst's reports to be submitted.

Saddharathana was expelled from the Buddhist monastic order. He had earlier been arrested by the CID over the alleged controversial statements and was released on bail. He is now in remand custody over a separate case linked to the tense situation at the sacred city of Anuradhapura.
