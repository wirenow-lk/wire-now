Kawanthissa’s death: Minister rejects news report attributing cause to diarrhoea

Society📍 Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-23 · Newswire

📌 What happened

Environment Minister Dammika Patabendi denied saying that elephant Kawanthissa died from diarrhoea. He said a daily Sri Lankan Sinhala-language newspaper report was inaccurate. He stressed that only a post-mortem report can determine the real cause of death.

📖 The full story

Environment Minister Dammika Patabendi issued a statement today (23) denying that he said elephant Kawanthissa died from diarrhoea. He was responding to a report in a daily Sri Lankan Sinhala-language newspaper that claimed the elephant's death was due to diarrhoea.

The minister said he never made that statement and that no one can decide the cause of death based on personal opinion. He said the cause, whether diarrhoea, a gunshot injury, or another reason, must be found through scientific and medical examinations. The final determination will come from the post-mortem report.

He warned that attributing false statements to him could spread misinformation and create public misconceptions. He asked the newspaper to correct the inaccurate report and share his real position with the public. He said the correction should have the same prominence as the original report.
