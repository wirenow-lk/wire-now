Nearly 200 major websites hit by supply-chain cyber attack: report

Tech🇱🇰 Sri Lanka · 2026-11-18 · 📰 Reported by 2 outlets

📌 What happened

A malicious cyber attack targeted nearly 200 major websites, including the BBC, HSBC UAE, Air France and IKEA. Hackers injected corrupted JavaScript code into Optimizely tools, affecting about 178 sites. The attackers aimed to steal credit card numbers and cryptocurrency and to infect Apple devices.

📖 The full story

Hackers injected corrupted JavaScript code into tools hosted by Optimizely, a digital platform used by major companies to customize online experiences. This malicious cyber attack targeted nearly 200 major websites, including the BBC, HSBC UAE, Air France and IKEA, as well as Zoom, CNN, Salesforce, GitLab, Equifax, Air France-KLM, Ledger and Peacock. The attack was reported Tuesday by specialist media.

The attackers sought to steal users’ credit card numbers and cryptocurrency and to upload code to Apple devices for further exploitation. According to security researcher Scott Helme, the attacker’s crypto wallets received about $7,040 within roughly nine hours on November 16. Helme first uncovered the hack and wrote about it on his blog Monday.

Most of the malicious code had been removed from the affected sites, Helme said. Optimizely stated that it identified and contained a security incident affecting some of its Web Experimentation customers. The company added that the vulnerable path was blocked, the injected code was removed, and customers were contacted directly.
