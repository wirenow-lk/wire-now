24 yr old STF officer killed in motorcycle accident in Matale

Society📍 Yatawatta, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-17 · Newswire

📌 What happened

A 24-year-old STF police officer was killed after his motorcycle crashed into a car and then hit a signboard on the Kurunegala–Matale Road in Yatawatta on Monday night. The impact left him with serious injuries, and he later died at Yatawatta Hospital. Police say the accident occurred when the bike collided with the rear of a vehicle traveling in the same direction.

📖 The full story

The deceased was a 24‑year-old Police Constable attached to the STF’s Parayanalankulam Camp and a resident of Yatawatta. He was riding a motorcycle toward Matale on the Kurunegala–Matale Road when his bike hit the rear of a car moving in the same direction.

After the collision the motorcycle veered off the road and struck a signboard outside a roadside shop, causing severe injuries that required hospital treatment. He was taken to Yatawatta Hospital where he later succumbed to his wounds, and the Yatawatta Police are now conducting further investigations.
