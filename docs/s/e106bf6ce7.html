Illegal vehicle possession: Johnston fined Rs. 130,000, handed suspended sentence

Politics📍 Colombo Fort, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-25 · 📰 Reported by 4 outlets

📌 What happened

Former Minister Johnston Fernando was fined a total of Rs. 130,000 and given a six-month suspended prison sentence by the Colombo Fort Magistrate's Court today (25) for illegally possessing a vehicle.

📖 The full story

Former Minister Johnston Fernando appeared at the Colombo Fort Magistrate's Court today (25) in a case about the illegal possession of a vehicle. The court found him guilty and imposed a total fine of Rs. 130,000 along with a six-month suspended prison sentence.

On the first charge, he was fined Rs. 5,000, with a three-month prison sentence if he does not pay. On the second charge, Fernando was fined Rs. 100,000 and sentenced to six months of simple imprisonment, suspended for five years.

On the third charge, the court fined him Rs. 25,000, with a three-month sentence of simple imprisonment in default of payment. The verdict and all penalties were reported by Newswire.
