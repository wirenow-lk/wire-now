Prior appointments mandatory for all DMT Narahenpita services from November 23

Politics📍 Narahenpita, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-19 · 📰 Reported by 3 outlets

📌 What happened

The Department of Motor Traffic will require prior appointments for all public services at its Narahenpita Head Office starting November 23, 2026. Daily appointment slots have been more than doubled to serve more people faster. People can book by phone or online and are asked not to queue outside early in the morning.

📖 The full story

The Department of Motor Traffic announced that from November 23, 2026, all public services at the Narahenpita Head Office will be provided only through a prior appointment system. Members of the public must book a date and time in advance. The department has increased daily appointment slots by more than double to handle more service seekers and ensure faster, more convenient delivery.

You can book an appointment by calling 011 2 117 116 or by visiting the department's online booking portal at https://dmtappointments.dmt.gov.lk. The public is urged not to queue unnecessarily outside the office from early morning beginning on that date. The Commissioner General of Motor Traffic, retired Air Vice Marshal Thushara Fernando, explained that the new system aims to improve public convenience and reduce waiting times.

He asked everyone seeking services at the Narahenpita Head Office to book ahead so they do not face inconvenience. The announcement came from Newswire.
