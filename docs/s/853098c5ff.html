Suspect arrested over assault on bus driver in Dambulla

Society📍 Dambulla, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-19 · Newswire

📌 What happened

A suspect has been arrested over the assault of a private bus driver in Dambulla. The attack happened on November 14 in front of the main bus stand, and footage of it circulated on social media. The suspect, from Pohoranwewa in Dambulla, was arrested yesterday (18) and is to be produced before court today (19).

📖 The full story

Police say the assault took place on November 14 in front of the main bus stand in Dambulla. It involved the driver of a private bus travelling from Kandy to Polonnaruwa, who was injured in the attack.

The injured driver lodged a complaint with Police while receiving treatment at the Dambulla Hospital. The arrest came after footage of the incident circulated on social media.

After investigations, officers of the Dambulla Headquarters Police used CCTV footage of the incident to identify the suspect. The suspect, a resident of Pohoranwewa in Dambulla, was arrested yesterday (18).

He is scheduled to be produced before court today (19). The Dambulla Headquarters Police are conducting further investigations.
