Rs. 451 billion, zero loans: Cabinet approves two new expressways

Politics📍 Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-16 · Newswire

📌 What happened

The Cabinet approved two new expressway projects worth Rs. 451 billion, to be built without loans using tax revenue. The projects are the Kurunegala–Galewela Expressway, the first section of Phase 4 of the Central Expressway, and the Kahathuduwa–Ingiriya Expressway, the first phase of the proposed Ruwanpura Expressway. Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake announced the approval in a social media post.

📖 The full story

According to Newswire, Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake said the government is undertaking major expressway development projects worth Rs. 451 billion. He made the announcement in a social media post.

The projects will be financed without any loans, using tax revenue to cover the costs. The minister described the investment as significant for road infrastructure, fully funded through tax revenue.

The Cabinet approved the Kurunegala–Galewela Expressway, which is the first section of Phase 4 of the Central Expressway. It also approved the Kahathuduwa–Ingiriya Expressway, the first phase of the proposed Ruwanpura Expressway.
