CPC overtime fraud case: Ananda Palitha further remanded

Society📍 Colombo, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-25 · 📰 Reported by 4 outlets

📌 What happened

D.D. Ananda Palitha, convenor of the Samagi Joint Trade Union Alliance, has been further remanded until December 4 over an alleged Rs. 3.1 million overtime allowance fraud at the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation. Colombo Additional Magistrate Liyan Warusha Vithana issued the order today (25) after submissions by the Central Crime Investigation Bureau. Palitha was arrested by the bureau on November 16.

📖 The full story

The case concerns alleged misappropriation of employee overtime allowances at the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC). Investigating officers told the court that the alleged fraud involved Rs. 3.1 million and took place over a five-year period from 2015 to 2019. Palitha is the convenor of the Samagi Joint Trade Union Alliance.

Colombo Additional Magistrate Liyan Warusha Vithana ordered the further remand until December 4. The order was made today (25) after submissions by the Central Crime Investigation Bureau. Palitha was arrested by the Central Crime Investigation Bureau on November 16 in connection with the investigation.
