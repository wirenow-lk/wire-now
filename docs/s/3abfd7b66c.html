📖 The full story

The Department of Meteorology reports a deep depression in the Bay of Bengal that is likely to intensify into a cyclonic storm by Monday (16) evening and further develop into a severe cyclonic storm by Tuesday (17). As of 11:30 p.m. on Sunday, the system was approximately 640 km northeast of Trincomalee and is expected to continue moving away from Sri Lanka.

The Meteorology Department says prevailing showers across Sri Lanka are expected to reduce from today. Several spells of showers are forecast in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, North-western and Southern provinces, as well as the Kandy and Nuwara Eliya districts. Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in Uva Province and Ampara District after 2 p.m. Mainly dry weather is expected elsewhere, with misty conditions possible in many areas during the early morning.

The public has been advised to take precautions against temporary strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
