Haitian Prime Minister commends Sri Lankan troops for successful anti-gang operation

📌 What happened

Sri Lankan troops serving with the international Gang Suppression Force joined Haiti's National Police in a pre-dawn raid on November 15 against the Kokorat San Ras gang in Gros-Morne, Artibonite. The joint forces freed a church and a school held by gang members, seized weapons and motorcycles, and cleared roads blocked for years. Haitian Prime Minister Alix Didier Fils-Aimé praised the operation on X.

📖 The full story

On November 15, Sri Lankan personnel serving under the international Gang Suppression Force (GSF) took part in a joint anti-gang operation with the Haitian National Police (PNH) in the Artibonite region. The pre-dawn raid targeted strongholds of the armed Kokorat San Ras gang in the Tibwadòm and Carnifice areas of Gros-Morne.

During the mission, the joint forces cleared and secured a church and a school that gang members had occupied. They seized firearms, ammunition, and motorcycles, and dismantled barricades and trenches that were blocking National Road 5. This cleared key roadways that had been blocked for years.

Haitian Prime Minister Alix Didier Fils-Aimé praised the coordinated operation in a post on X, commending the PNH, GSF and Sri Lankan troops. Sri Lankan personnel are stationed across key operational hubs including Port-au-Prince, Gonaïves, and Estrella. There they conduct armoured vehicle patrols, safeguard vital supply routes, and support local authorities in dismantling organized crime networks and restoring stability for affected civilian communities.

💡 Why it matters

The raid reopened roads that had been blocked for years and helped restore stability for local communities affected by gangs.
