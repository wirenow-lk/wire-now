Deadly day on roads: Four killed in Malabe, Mount Lavinia and Eastern Province

Society📍 Malabe, Mount Lavinia, Eravur and Sammanthurai, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-21 · Newswire

📌 What happened

Four people died in four separate road accidents across Sri Lanka on Friday (20), police said. The dead included a 79-year-old woman from Ratmalana and a 23-year-old motorcyclist from Malwatta 02. The crashes were reported in Malabe, Mount Lavinia, Eravur and Sammanthurai.

📖 The full story

Police said four people were killed in four different road accidents on Friday (20), in Malabe, Mount Lavinia, Eravur and Sammanthurai. The victims included a 79-year-old woman and a 23-year-old motorcyclist.

In Mount Lavinia, a 79-year-old woman from Ratmalana was hit by a car while she was crossing the Galle–Colombo Road. She was badly injured and taken to Kalubowila Hospital, where she died later. Police arrested the car driver.

On the Batticaloa–Trincomalee Road, a 42-year-old motorcyclist from Manampitiya died in a crash involving several vehicles. Police said a lorry heading towards Valaichchenai hit two motorcycles travelling in front of it, then crashed into a car near the Kattupalli Road junction. Both motorcyclists were badly hurt and taken to Eravur Hospital, where one of them died, and the lorry driver was arrested.

On the Kalmunai–Ampara Road, a 23-year-old motorcyclist from Malwatta 02 died after hitting a lorry coming from the opposite direction; the motorcyclist and the pillion rider were taken to Ampara Hospital with serious injuries, and the rider later died of his injuries. Separately, a pedestrian was struck by a motorcycle running along Wanaguru Mawatha from Horahena Junction towards Hokandara Junction; both were badly hurt and taken to Mulleriyawa Hospital, where the pedestrian died, and police said the pedestrian has not yet been identified. The police stations concerned are still investigating all four accidents.
