📖 The full story

The Island reports that 257 Women Police Constables have been recruited by the police to strengthen protection for women and children and raise female representation in the force. The new recruits were admitted to Nikaweratiya Police College on Monday (23) to begin approximately six months of basic police training.

Training will include both theory and practice, covering law, police duties, physical fitness, disaster management, public relations, leadership and personality development, and firearms training. The senior management of the Police College will closely monitor the health and welfare of the trainees throughout the course.

Each recruit will receive the monthly government salary for a Police Constable starting from the date of admission to the college. Police said the goal is to give greater priority to the safety of women and children and to strengthen counselling services that help prevent crime.
