📖 The full story

Trump is set to headline his third crypto gala on Sunday night at Trump National Golf Club in Sterling, Virginia. The event rewards top holders of his $TRUMP meme coin and includes meet-and-greets with Floyd Mayweather, Kevin O’Leary, Tristan Thompson and Lior Suchard, according to its website. Access is split by tiers for 185 holders: 120 attendees get a Trump fragrance, poster, “commemorative” ticket and “Trump watch”; the top 29 get a VIP reception; the top four get a “TRUMP 18K Gold Watch.”

Democrats say Trump is improperly profiting from his crypto ventures and have criticized him and his family for monetizing his brand through equities and crypto while he is in office. After sweeping wins in the midterms, Democrats vowed to investigate conflicts of interest involving Trump and his personal wealth during his presidency and to push for legislation banning activities such as the meme coin gala dinner. Last month, Adam Schiff and eight other US senators sent a letter saying the dinner and scoring system appeared built to increase investment in $TRUMP and let the president abuse his office to enrich himself.

Since returning to the White House, Trump has made more than $1bn from his crypto businesses, according to financial disclosures released in June. He received more than $500m from World Liberty Financial selling new crypto products and another $600m from sales of souvenir-type “meme” coins stamped with his face; he launched the $Trump meme coin days before his inauguration. The White House did not immediately respond to a request for comment, while White House spokesperson Anna Kelly told Reuters earlier this year that neither the president nor his family has ever engaged or will ever engage in conflicts of interest and that Trump proudly made the United States the crypto capital of the world through executive actions.

Interest in the $TRUMP dinner has fallen, with contestants’ combined holdings dropping from $148m in May 2025 to $8.6m in this month’s gala; an analysis of blockchain data for Reuters by Public Citizen found more than half of the digital wallets publicly listed on the contest leaderboard for Sunday’s dinner are tied directly to crypto exchanges that bar US-based users, indicating links to foreign nationals. The Trump family’s other crypto-connected equities and ventures have fallen to record lows, and outside investors in their five main crypto and crypto-linked projects have lost more than $5bn, according to a Reuters analysis. The meme coin has earned the Trump family at least $635m, according to the president’s June financial disclosure statement; in total, the Trumps had earned at least $2.3bn through April from their four main crypto ventures, including the meme coin, and the president’s stake in a fifth crypto-linked venture, Trump Media & Technology Group, is worth about $430m.
