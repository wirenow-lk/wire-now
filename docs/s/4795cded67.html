Rs. 1,010 million vs. Rs. 31.7 million: Minister reveals foreign travel costs of Ranil and AKD

Politics📍 Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-22 · Newswire

📌 What happened

Minister Nalinda Jayatissa told Parliament that former President Ranil Wickremesinghe spent Rs. 1,010 million on 33 foreign trips during his 26 months in office. By comparison, President Anura Kumara Dissanayake spent Rs. 31.7 million on eight foreign trips over a similar period.

📖 The full story

Minister Nalinda Jayatissa shared the figures in Parliament on Saturday, November 21. He said former President Ranil Wickremesinghe spent Rs. 1,010 million on 33 foreign trips during his 26 months in office.

According to the minister, 421 people went with Wickremesinghe on those 33 trips. The trips stretched from his visit to the United Kingdom on September 17, 2022, to his visit to India on June 8, 2024.

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake made eight foreign trips in his first 26 months, costing Rs. 31.7 million in total. The minister added that the current President did not claim any extra or combined allowances for those visits.
