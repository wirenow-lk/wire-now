Foreign Ministry coordinates repatriation of remains of two Sri Lankan workers killed in South Korea

Society📍 Hwaseong, South Korea 🇰🇷 · 2026-11-28 · 📰 Reported by 3 outlets

📌 What happened

Two Sri Lankan migrant workers died in a road accident in Hwaseong, South Korea, at around 9 p.m. on Nov. 26, 2026. The Sri Lankan Embassy in Seoul was informed by Korean police on Nov. 27. Sri Lanka’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism is coordinating with South Korean authorities and the families to repatriate the remains, Acting Minister Arun Hemachandra said.

📖 The full story

Acting Minister of Foreign Affairs Arun Hemachandra said the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism is working with South Korean authorities and the families of the two Sri Lankan migrant workers. The aim is to facilitate the return of their remains after the road accident in Hwaseong, South Korea.

The accident happened at around 9 p.m. on Nov. 26, 2026. The Sri Lankan Embassy in Seoul was officially told by Korean police on Nov. 27.

The Embassy’s Consular Section is coordinating the required formalities with relevant authorities, the ministry and the families. It is doing this to speed up the repatriation process and to give consular and welfare assistance.

Hemachandra said both workers had gone to South Korea after registering with the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE).
