16-year-old dies after car crashes into bus stop in Kohuwala

Society📍 Kohuwala, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-24 · Newswire

📌 What happened

A 16-year-old died after a car went out of control and hit a bus stop on S. de S. Jayasinghe Mawatha in Kohuwala on Monday evening (Nov. 23). The car had been travelling from Nugegoda towards Kohuwala and hit a motorcycle and the rear of another car before striking the child at the bus stop. The child was taken to Colombo South Teaching Hospital in Kalubowila but died, and the 68-year-old driver was arrested.

📖 The full story

Police said the car was going from Nugegoda towards Kohuwala when it lost control. It first hit a motorcycle and the back of another car. The car then hit the child, who was at a nearby bus stop, before hitting another motorcycle, a car and a van.

The child was seriously injured and was admitted to the Colombo South Teaching Hospital in Kalubowila. The child later died from the injuries. Three other people hurt in the accident were also admitted to the hospital.

The dead child was a resident of the Nugegoda area. The car driver, aged 68, is a resident of Morawila in Panadura and has been arrested. Kohuwala Police are carrying out further investigations.
