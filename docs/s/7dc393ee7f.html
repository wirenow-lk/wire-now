Foreign-Flagged Suspected Drug Boat Seized

Society📍 Dondra, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-27 · Newsfirst

📌 What happened

The Sri Lanka Navy caught seven suspects and seized a boat believed to be carrying heroin and crystal methamphetamine (ICE) in international waters south of Dondra. The search went beyond Sri Lanka's Exclusive Economic Zone. The stateless boat and the seven suspects are now being taken ashore.

📖 The full story

The Sri Lanka Navy caught seven suspects and took control of a boat thought to be carrying heroin and crystal methamphetamine, also called ICE. This happened during a search in international waters south of Dondra, in an area past Sri Lanka's Exclusive Economic Zone. The boat had no nationality, so it was stateless.

The Navy said it ran the operation together with local and foreign intelligence agencies and maritime partners. The stateless boat and the seven suspects are now being escorted to shore. There they will be handed to the Police Narcotic Bureau for more investigations and legal proceedings.

The Navy said closer coordination with regional and international maritime partners and stronger surveillance made the operation work. Its Information Fusion Centre in Colombo pulls together data from modern tools, including satellites, to judge intelligence and sea threats. Joint aerial surveillance with the Sri Lanka Air Force has widened how much ocean it can watch.

This seizure came under the government's 'A Nation United' anti-narcotics campaign, started in October 2025. Under that campaign, the Navy has seized drugs with an estimated street value above Rs. 116 billion. Since January 2026, it has handed over 66 boats, including four foreign vessels, and 379 suspects, including 18 foreign nationals, for legal proceedings over drug smuggling. The Navy said more details about the latest seizure will come later.
