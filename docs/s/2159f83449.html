18-year-old Nethya shines with gold for Sri Lanka ‎

Sports📍 Bhubaneswar, India, Sri Lanka 🇱🇰 · 2026-11-26 · The Island

📌 What happened

Eighteen-year-old Nethya Sampath cleared 2.08 metres to win Sri Lanka's first gold at the BIMSTEC Under-23 Athletics Championships in Bhubaneswar, India. He did it in one attempt and had a personal best of 2.13 metres. Dineth Induwara Weeraratna also won gold in the men's 100 metres.

📖 The full story

Nethya Sampath, an eighteen-year-old high jumper from Maris Stella College Thimbirigaskatuwa, won Sri Lanka's first gold. He did this at the BIMSTEC Under-23 Athletics Championships in Bhubaneswar, India, which started on Wednesday.

Nethya cleared 2.08 metres in one attempt, and his personal best is 2.13 metres. India's Binoy and Ambriesh also cleared 2.08 metres but needed more than one attempt.

Dineth Induwara Weeraratna won the men's 100 metres in 10.35 seconds. India's Rushiprasad Desai was second, and Sri Lanka's Saad Jamaldeen was third. Sri Lanka also had a runner-up finish in the 1500 metres.
