📖 The full story

A data entry officer at a private company has been arrested over an online banking fraud involving Rs. 1 million. The Wariyapola Computer Crime Investigation Division began investigating after a person complained that Rs. 1 million had been taken from his bank account.

The investigation found that the victim was tricked into giving his bank account login details through a fraudulent website. The fake site was designed to look like the official website of a private bank.

Using information from the investigation, officers arrested the suspect around noon yesterday (26). The suspect is 25 years old and lives in Ragama. The Wariyapola Computer Crime Investigation Division is conducting further investigations.
