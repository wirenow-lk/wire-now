Minister orders urgent probe after mass fish deaths reported at Kilinochchi Tank

Fisheries Minister Ramalingam Chandrasegar has ordered an urgent investigation into mass fish deaths at Kilinochchi Tank. The directive followed a telephone discussion with Northern Province Governor Nagalingam Vedanayagam. Fish, freshwater prawns and juvenile fish reportedly died in the lower section of the tank.

📖 The full story

Fisheries Minister Ramalingam Chandrasegar ordered an urgent probe after large numbers of fish, freshwater prawns and juvenile fish died in the lower part of Kilinochchi Tank. He issued the directive after speaking by telephone with Northern Province Governor Nagalingam Vedanayagam. The minister also called for a report on the incident.

Concerns have been raised over water-release procedures and possible problems during irrigation operations. Chandrasegar requested an assessment of financial losses suffered by freshwater fishing families who lost fish stocks and fishing equipment. An environmental assessment was also ordered to determine the impact on water quality and the surrounding ecosystem.

The minister said the causes of the fish deaths must be identified. He called for appropriate measures to provide relief to affected fishing communities.
